# 素材说明

大厅横幅使用内置 ImageGen 生成，为棋魂项目定制。成品为 `public/xiangqi-hero.webp`，使用 WebP 保存以减少体积。

## 最终提示词

> Create a wide 2.4:1 editorial still-life hero photograph for a refined Chinese chess app. A pale honey bamboo Chinese xiangqi board, viewed at a low oblique angle, with a few finely crafted round wooden xiangqi tokens with red and charcoal engravings. Directional afternoon window light, soft bamboo-leaf shadows and an off-white sage-green studio backdrop, quietly tactile and refreshing. Place most of the board and tokens in the right two thirds, with an airy pale sage left third that blends smoothly into #e2efdd. Crop interestingly at right edges. Photorealistic woodgrain with understated magazine art direction, medium contrast, no text overlays, no watermark, no logos. This is an atmospheric background photo, not a playable arrangement. Landscape.

棋盘线、落点、棋子和 Android 图标由程序绘制。背景乐和走棋音效由 Web Audio 合成，随应用离线运行。

## v0.2 斗兽棋棋子

八种动物棋子用内置 ImageGen 生成，保留透明度并编码为 `public/jungle-animals.webp`。渲染时按四列两行定位，棋子同时提供中文小标识。

> Generate one production sprite atlas for colorful animal head icons for a Chinese Jungle chess mobile game. Transparent background. A precise, regular 4 columns by 2 rows grid of 8 separate head-and-neck animal illustrations. Each illustration centered in its own perfectly equal cell, with at least 15% transparent margin on all sides. Order top row left to right: gray mouse with round pink ears, cream orange house cat with pointed ears, gray wolf with a longer muzzle, floppy-eared golden dog. Bottom row left to right: golden leopard with clear dark rosettes, orange tiger with black stripes, tawny lion with full mane, cool gray elephant with trunk and large ears. Modern children’s nature field guide, gentle dimensional matte paint, clean distinctive silhouettes, front or subtle three-quarter view, natural color palette, readable at 32 pixels. No plates, no circles, no background wash, no labels, no lettering, no accessories, no shadows outside the icons. 2:1 landscape canvas, all eight icons equal in scale and fully separated.

## v0.3 棋室与牌桌图赏

使用内置 ImageGen 制作四幅图赏。按生成网格分割并保存为 `public/art/xiangqi-studio.webp`、`go-garden.webp`、`landlord-velvet.webp` 与 `shedding-leather.webp`。

> A premium art atlas for a refined Chinese tabletop game app, exactly four equal rectangular images tiled in a precise 2 by 2 grid, no gutters, no shared objects between quadrants. Landscape overall. Each quadrant is independently composed and its hero objects are wholly contained within its own frame. Top left: Chinese xiangqi round wooden discs on an elegant dark walnut river board, jade silk, strong directional evening light, tactile engraved wood, quiet Chinese scholar studio. Top right: black and white Go stones and a small open ceramic bowl on a clean pale yellow wood go board, teal garden reflections, zen editorial still life. Bottom left: a small spread of classic ivory French-suited playing cards, heart, club and spade pips, one restrained gold jack illustration, on deep dark forest green velvet with raking light, refined art-deco corner hairlines. Bottom right: an ivory poker fan with a few oxblood red and black pips on a warm dark red leather tabletop, dark wood edges, assertive but refined. All four visual pieces cinematic photorealistic still life, art-directed, beautifully tactile, coherent dark-green and warm cream palette across the atlas, sharp clean edges for cropping. No people, no UI, no brand names, no logo, no text overlays, no watermarks. Render cleanly and avoid any dividers.

标题使用 Noto Serif SC 的本地字形子集，字体授权随 `public/art/Noto-Serif-SC-OFL.txt` 分发。界面控件和小字号正文沿用系统字体。

普通话语音使用本机 Windows Kangkang 普通话语音合成 40 条短 WAV，修整静音与峰值后保存于 `public/voice`。运行时从本地包读取，不需要联网或安装相同的发音人。木子、牌面和撞击音仍由 Web Audio 合成。

## v0.4 配乐与反馈

本版三首原创循环配乐由 `tools/render_soundtrack.py` 编排，铃音、拨弦、低音和鼓组以数值合成后写入 `public/music`。竹影清音 88 BPM、楚河交锋 116 BPM、牌桌律动 122 BPM。三首都使用立体声，循环边界保留短混响。重新生成时需要 Python、NumPy 和 SoundFile。

默认可在设置中开启自动随局配乐，棋盘和牌桌转场淡入淡出，语音与重音触发时降低背景音乐。重新合成和修剪部分普通话短句，并加入升王、连跳、同花顺、获胜和惜败等。共有 48 个离线报音文件。

## v0.5 棋具图赏与全馆报音

内置 ImageGen 为五子棋、英式跳棋、飞行棋和锄大地生成四幅图赏，分割压缩成 `stone-inlay.webp`、`crowned-walnut.webp`、`flight-route.webp`、`plum-deck.webp`。棋线、棋子和落点由程序绘制。标题字体补齐全部棋种和牌桌名称使用的字形，在小屏保留统一字重。

> One landscape editorial atlas of four equal independent 2x2 rectangular photographs, exact no-gap grid. Product art for a refined Chinese game pavilion app. Top left, polished pale-gold Japanese style black-white stones set in a cream rosewood board with bamboo and quiet late-afternoon window shadows, a few crossing lines only. Top right, closeup of lacquered oblique walnut checkers board with two deep-red and jet black carved discs and one crowned disc, warm studio light, refined tactile detail. Bottom left, miniature planes in cream, vermilion and jade paint lined on old folded paper route map, lively but tasteful warm sun, no printed text. Bottom right, 3 ivory poker cards fanned on plum velvet, sharply etched club/spade/diamond pips and small subtle gold embossing, art deco atmosphere. Natural material colors, consistent editorial lens, well-lit focal objects contained within their own quadrant, clearly separated motifs. No text overlays, watermarks or logos. Clean image art, no UI.

新增 89 段短句，共 137 个离线普通话 WAV。新增台词记录在 `src/voice-lines.ts`，用 Windows Kangkang 合成 22.05 kHz、16 位单声道，修剪静音、短淡入淡出并归一化。完整模式播报棋种常见动作、牌型和部分点数。快速行棋时，等待中的普通报音只保留最新一条，关键着法和结算优先。连牌使用较短的纸面扫动声，炸弹、王炸和同花顺有各自的重音和台面演出。
